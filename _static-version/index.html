Our Story

Sun in the cup,
bean at the heart.

Sol is the sun — the warmth, the morning light, the people who walk in. Bean is the craft — sourced with care, roasted with patience, brewed with intent. Put them together and you get a coffee house built for slow mornings and long conversations.

We pull every shot like it matters, bake in small batches, and keep the playlist low enough for you to hear the person across the table.
